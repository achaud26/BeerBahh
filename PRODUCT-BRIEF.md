# BeerBahh product brief

Filled by founder — Jul 2026. Source of truth for product decisions.

## 1. Vision & vibe

**One sentence:** A nightlife radar to let students know if going out is the right move and how the bars are feeling.

**Friday 9pm feeling:** Hyped — either add FOMO or cure FOMO.

**Never feel like:** Creepy, fake, a joke. It's the **WAZE of going out**.

**Today's alternatives suck because:** Inaccurate word of mouth; Snap/IG aren't visible to everyone; people lie. Want AI mods in chat keeping info relevant + a more secure/trustworthy system.

## 2. Who it's for

- **#1 user:** College students — Greek and non-Greek. Wants visibility into rented-out bars.
- **Later:** Bars (deals/ads), anyone going out.
- **Not for in v1:** No one excluded.
- **First 50–100:** Friends + known people.

> **Risk note (product):** Founder mentioned underage users + cop awareness. Cop/LEO alerts stay **out of product** until legal + App Store policy review. Rented-out / private-event flags are in scope.

## 3. Features

**Must at launch**

- Map
- Crowd levels
- Live chat that feels like YouTube livestream
- Accounts + personal profiles

**Soon after**

- Notifications
- Bars joining + posting drink deals

**Later:** Other college towns if it picks up.

**Hard no:** Dating — this is for going out only.

**Crowd updates:** AI reads chat and adjusts levels; maybe location later.

**Chat:** Usernames; friends can see more; not age-gated; AI moderation.

## 4. Bars & content

- Spot list: current set is fine.
- **Bar pitch:** "Want more foot traffic any night with just a message? Use our app to bring in more customers."
- Bars edit their own profile (anything).
- **First 3 to lock:** TD's, Wien's (Itsurwiener), Study Hall — want the most traffic.

## 5. Brand & design

- **Logo:** Sheep with glasses + beer — Duolingo-energy mascot.
- **Colors:** Black nightlife base + white/green sheep accents.
- **Voice refs:** Duolingo (mascot), Waze (notifications), Twitch (live chat). Own vibe otherwise.
- **Hate vibe:** GroupMe / Snap / IG — more about bars, less about people.
- Focus messaging on main downtown bars (not Esso-centric).

## 6. Product & screens

1. Not logged in → login / create account
2. Logged in → **map** with top question: **"where's the herd going?"**
3. Optional feeling check: how are you feeling about going out → options
4. **Auth:** Google login + profile (private unless friends)
5. **Platforms:** iOS + Android at launch (target)
6. **Alerts:** Thu–Sat going-out prompts; if "going out" → hourly bar updates

## 7. Go-to-market

- Launch: fall or spring semester
- Hype: on-campus marketing
- Assets: a couple frats + bouncers for bar intros
- Success: total users + how active nights feel / nights getting better

## 8. Business

- Students: free forever
- Bars: pay to market / promoted deals
- Later: student paid drink-deal access possible
- Budget: nothing yet

## 9. Team & ops

- Co-founder: Jason; recruit business students
- Moderation: AI
- Time: a couple hours/week (college)

## 10. Fears

Biggest fear: flops because students think it's a joke / abuse the chat.
